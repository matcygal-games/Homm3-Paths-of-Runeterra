PILTOVER HERO ART CANDIDATES, 25 OCTOBER 2026

Status: painted-master-complete-unapproved. No native gameplay, interface,
final external art or redistribution approval is claimed.

Fifteen distinct hero portrait masters were each generated in a separate built-in
OpenAI imagegen call. Full prompts and sanitized reference ancestry are in each
hero's generation-request.json. Lymere/Uberti appearances are project fiction;
Mir's name/body association and Kepple's female presentation are explicit project
interpretations. The other named appearances were guided by inspected private
official references without reusing their illustration pixels.

Specialty masters use eleven separately generated painted motif images, with one
survey-spyglass image intentionally shared by Ezreal's sight and Jae's Scouting.
Heimerdinger, Jayce and Zevi specialties derive from the project's own Freight
Handler, living Dockyard Lifter and Academy Artificer art. Their exact technical
crop/own-background ancestry is in specialty-generation-request.json.

Every hero has large.png (58x64), small.png (48x32), specialtyLarge.png (44x44)
and specialtySmall.png (32x32), all RGBA. Corresponding raster candidate recipes
bind the exact native source, canvas and cached painted-master SHA-256.

Reproduce all sixty native files and recipes from the cached own masters:
  python assets/source/heroes/painted-v1/derive_native.py

The coordinator selects canonical recipes and publishes stages. This candidate
producer does not write a live stage or select its own work for release.

Public PNG metadata and JSON ancestry omit private workstation/provider paths.
Metadata stripping was checked with exact decoded RGBA byte equality. Raw
provider requests/hints/original PNGs and licensed reference pictures remain in
private coordinator reports outside public source bundles. Cached independently
painted masters, technical sources and recipes are retained here for replay.
