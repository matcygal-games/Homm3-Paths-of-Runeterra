## Shape to preserve

The actual icon is a **bilaterally symmetric red, axe-like war crest**. Its two outer sides are broad hooked/crescent blades, which curve inward near the upper edge and surround two rounded dark openings. A narrow, upright central spine connects angular inward-notched middle sections to a pointed lower structure. Long dark channels separate the lower tapered portions. The contour has sharp tips and concave cuts: neither a solid red shield nor a bird, rose, star or ordinary two-stroke letter.

Use the supplied private reference itself to resolve the exact contour. The geometric description is an artist's visual reading, not a canonical explanation of its symbolism. Do not invent official meanings for the individual blades or turn the rounded negative spaces into eyes of a literal skull.

**It must not become Demacia's blade-and-feather emblem.** Preserve the Noxian hooked outer contour and paired negative spaces. Do not add layered feather rows, a winged upright sword, Demacian royal blue or a generic unrelated coat of arms merely because Demacia's painted sprite treatment is the style reference.

## Independent production treatment

- Repaint the regional shape as a worn cloth stamp, shield/buckler paint, small embossed plate or branded harness badge. Original icon pixels are reference inputs, not sprites to paste onto the model.
- Use a restrained material-appropriate red, pale weathered paint or dark iron contrast against the unit's selected cloth/armor. Preserve the contour in a single-color treatment; the icon's screen gradient is not mandatory on fabric or metal.
- Prioritize the silhouette, paired upper cutouts and central axis at actual native size. A badge too small to preserve any negative space must be moved to a larger readable surface or clearly marked unresolved, rather than accepted as an arbitrary red smudge.
- Keep the badge attached to one plausible surface and match the surface's perspective, folds, light and wear. It should move with that same armor/cloth/harness through the complete action set, not float in screen coordinates.
- Bases may use a plain, chipped stamped patch; upgrades may use a larger, more legible edged or embossed version of the **same** emblem. Better heraldry does not replace the selected body/equipment progression.
- Animals, engines and large beings need an actual harness, pennant or service plate where appropriate. Do not invent a natural skin pattern, tattoo or biological marking as canonical Noxian anatomy.
- The current rig/body emblem surface may remain blank while portraits and other independent work proceed. This reference does not authorize overwriting another worker's selected master or declaring body/action heraldry finished.
