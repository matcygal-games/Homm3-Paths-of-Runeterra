The producer verifies original painted public-source and whole-actor alpha matte
hashes before transforming any pixels. It does not call an image service, render
3D, draw actor geometry, warp limbs or synthesize missing actions. All creative
pixels were generated using the built-in imagegen tool; exact prompts and raw/
public decoded-pixel equality receipts are supplied. Original provider PNGs and
licensed reference images are preserved privately and are not reproduction inputs.

All source atlases use whole actors isolated by exact eight-connected alpha32
mattes. This removes detached transparent noise and actual adjacent-cell fragments
without changing painted RGB. Each atlas has one constant paper-density calibration;
poses retain explicit source roots, native450x400 canvas and root197266. No frame
is fitted independently to its opaque bounds. Shadows belong to the painted actor.
