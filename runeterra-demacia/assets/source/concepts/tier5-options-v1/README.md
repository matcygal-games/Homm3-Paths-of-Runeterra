# Alternative unit-line quick renders

Created 19 October 2026 with the built-in image generation tool. Five separate comparison renders show the unupgraded unit on the left and upgraded unit on the right. Exact prompts and reference roles are in `prompts.json`; preserved output dimensions, SHA-256 values, cache origins and review limits are in `generation.json`. `comparison-sheet-v1.jpg` is a technical contact sheet of those unchanged masters.

These are design proposals. They are not registered creatures, approved native sprites, animation sheets or evidence of in-game rendering. The artwork has larger modern concept detail that still needs translation and review at Heroes III native scale. There is no chosen replacement roster yet.

1. **Resonance Adept -> Harmonic Warden:** invented grounded Demacian musician using a strapped dulcimer; ranged musical support is proposed. Inspired by Sona's music theme, with independent equipment, hair, clothing and silhouette. No copied Etwahl or established canonical military order is claimed. In the selected Mageseeker era, public official magical musician recruitment needs narrative justification. This alternative can retain melee Mageseekers.
2. **Dragonguard -> Veteran Dragonguard:** anti-monster polearm infantry; plain mail becomes reinforced plate, drake-hide mantle and improved polearm. Recruitment/abilities and this exact upgrade are adaptations.
3. **Silverwing Rider -> Silverwing Skyguard:** two-legged feathered raptor mount with a rider; increased armor, tack and weapon quality. Flight would be a deliberate change to the earlier grounded roster, requiring balance and full flying animations.
4. **Petricite Bolt Thrower -> Royal Siege Engine:** invented crewed mechanical weapon; exposed timber carriage becomes reinforced protective mantlet and heavier mechanism. Recruitment as a stack, ammunition and AI behavior remain proposals.
5. **Badgerbear Handler -> Armored Badgerbear Handler:** stocky gray-white beast beside a small Yordle handler, rather than ridden cavalry; substantial fitted armor distinguishes the upgrade. This exact armored recruitment line is an adaptation.

Primary subject references: Riot's [Sona profile](https://www.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/champions/sona/), [Demacia region](https://universe.leagueoflegends.com/en_US/region/demacia/) and official Legends of Runeterra Data Dragon cards. Private original LoR images and exact metadata/hashes remain under `references/concept-options/official-lor-references.json`, outside project-generated source art. Licensed Heroes III Halberdier pixels were used only as a rendering-language reference; the project's Guardian master supplied the Demacian palette reference. No reference artwork is bundled in this directory.

The user has not selected a tier-five alternative. The earlier authored ranged Mageseeker experiment is provisional and must not be presented as a final coherent roster while melee/ranged roles and tier five are being decided.
