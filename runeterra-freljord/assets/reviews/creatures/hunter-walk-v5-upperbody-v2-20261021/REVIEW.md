broader silver bodice and reinforced bow. It preserves the broad alternating
near/far contact order. However, it enlarges the figures and mispacks the rows:
lower-row hoods intrude into the top row's fixed 512-pixel cells, while fixed
bottom-row crops cut the tops of those hoods. All three top-row alpha boxes touch
source Y512, and the bottom row starts at source-relative Y0. The first cell's
inspection crop visibly contains a fragment of the next-row hood at its lower
edge. This is a complete-cohort layout failure and violates the requested fixed
lower-body/camera/layout invariants.

The twelve raster files in v2 native-candidates are INSPECTION OUTPUTS, not a
delivered repaired pair. Veteran diagnostic exports deliberately retain the
fixed cell boundaries and show the ghost/clipping problem. No masking,
inpainting, component collage, per-row/per-frame recentering or invented missing
pixels are used to conceal it. The Veteran v2 cohort remains rejected and
unselected. A new coherent source with proper margins and the selected identity
