RUNETERRA REGIONAL HERALDRY - 25 OCTOBER 2026

User requirement: "make sure that all the regions has visable crestets on their towns and their units".

SCOPE
Every implemented Runeterra region, every town construction progression, and each of its fourteen base/upgraded troop variants. New regional implementations and later replacement artwork inherit this requirement. Original Heroes III towns/resources remain untouched. Planned regions without native assets are pending implementations, not visually verified regions.

CORRECT IDENTITY
Use the selected region's documented crest, with a primary-source shape reference, exact URL/access date and image SHA-256. Distinguish regional heraldry from champion, dynasty, tribe, company, religious-order and combined LoR deck-region symbols. Those may be secondary details when appropriate; they do not silently replace the regional marker. A guessed URL, filename, generation prompt or palette is not evidence of the visible emblem. Do not redistribute private official-reference PNGs as project artwork or treat a generated derivative as rights clearance.

TOWNS
Integrate recognizable regional markings into large readable civic banners, gates, stone/metal reliefs, institutions or culturally appropriate painted graffiti. Use more than one sensible location in a large city. Check empty/early, partial and fully built states, base/upgrade replacements, coast and all accessible viewpoints. The only crest must not vanish behind a later building or lie on a hidden layer. Match architecture, lighting, materials, weathering and perspective. Preserve approved building masters/layouts unless a specific crest edit is needed; do not repaint a coherent city to add heraldry.

UNITS
All seven base/upgrade pairs need visible regional affiliation, including modest base units. Use shields, breastplates, tabards, cloth, practical harnesses/collars, ornaments or other credible local attachments. For spirits, animals and non-state communities, record regional association as a gameplay/art adaptation; never claim that a marking proves canonical state ownership, shared religion or political obedience. Do not tattoo a crest onto bare animal flesh merely to fill a checkbox. Upgrades should retain the recognizable identity and improve equipment/materials, not just enlarge a logo.

Review battle holding/movement and genuine attacks/reactions/turns/death, both facings, adventure appearances and portrait/recruitment crops. At least one ordinary native battle view must make the emblem recognizable; a large portrait or separate UI faction icon alone cannot satisfy it. Natural pose occlusion is correct. A floating always-front-facing stamp, artwork pasted above an occluding limb, cell bleed, changed roots or a new idle substituted for an action is not correct.

PRODUCTION
Painted 2D original-HoMM3-compatible artwork only. No Blender/equivalent3D, original troop recolours, code-drawn substitute characters or geometric icon patches presented as finished painting. Use the imagegen skill/built-in image editing for creative bitmap corrections. Technical conversion/cropping, fixed placement, masking and assembly may preserve untouched pixels around selected genuinely painted edits. Keep originals, raw provider receipts, exact prompts, selected sources and checksums. Do not reactivate retired designs or run historical producers.

INTEGRATION
Changing a selected master requires the actual producer, all affected action/portrait/map outputs, recipes, manifest, generated resources, stage and review hashes to agree. Do not fix a gallery screenshot while the mounted mod keeps old art. Do not mark only new idle pictures complete when all action frames are stale. Hold source/stage during native tests. Rebuild through the existing regional dispatcher and run focused conversion/semantic/freshness checks before native verification; keep unrelated gameplay statistics/economy unchanged.

EVIDENCE
Keep a machine-readable ledger per actual region and variant: crest identity/reference hash, selected resource and exact runtime hash, animation frame map, native dimensions, surface/position, named reviewer, native-size findings, observed source/native scope and remaining correction. Missing/unbuilt outputs, absent crests, unreadable approximations and unreviewed frames are pending, never implied passes. A report generator or hash validator checks linkage only; inspect the pixels and game. Source review does not close motion, final human art or redistribution acceptance. Preserve old build-bound evidence as history.
