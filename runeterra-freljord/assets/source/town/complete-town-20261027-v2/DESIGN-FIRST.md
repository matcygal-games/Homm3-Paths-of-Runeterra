# Freljord complete-town design checkpoint

Status: **design proposal** while the full painted master is reviewed.

The latest user direction rejects the existing town's arrangement and requires
the entire completed town to be designed first, before separating any background
or building layers. This successor deliberately does not freeze the previous
V1 slots, row arrangement, street routes or active geometry.

Design one inhabited small northern/Viking-like village in a pine-lined mountain
hollow, with aurora and one irregular shared common/hearth. Related timber
longhouses, working buildings, domestic shelters and ritual institutions form
organic neighborhoods along connected packed-snow streets. Grand rear halls and
the stronghold retain real physical mass; apparent foreground scale frames the
view without turning it into an aerial/isometric board. Doors, full silhouettes,
stairs, roofs, foundations and their surroundings are solved in the same painting.

The first complete master contains sixteen ordinary dry-land functional sites:

1. High Council Hall: monumental civic longhouse and carved regional crest.
2. Winter Stronghold: substantial timber/stone gate/palisade institution.
3. Lorekeeper Circle: northern archive lodge, no pagoda.
4. Warmother's Hall / Wayfarer Longhouse: communal hearth/tavern institution.
5. Caravan Market: one grouped stall/trading site beside the common.
6. Forge of Ornn / Supply Smithy: working forge and chimney.
7. True Ice Quarry: restrained crystal loading/hoist yard.
8. Anivia's Shrine: stone bird relief/standing stone and offering approach.
9. Frostguard Vigil: a distinct small cold-cloth ritual lodge.
10. Fluft Gathering / Poro Shelter: low shelter and connected yard.
11. Veteran Hunters' Lodge: practical hunting lodge.
12. Rime Troll Cavern: natural peripheral cave site.
13. Inquisitor Enclave / Draklorn Watch: substantial Frostguard practitioner hall.
14. Winter Veil Sanctum / Starlit Lodge: distinct diviner's lodge.
15. Outrider Stockade: covered druvask stable and practical pens.
16. Rimewake Eyrie: one grounded side/back snow-rock roost/perch complex.

No ordinary site stands in water or on an unsupported cliff/void. The optional
coast and additive Grail remain explicit later branches, absent from the first
sixteen-land-site master. The same service/replacement relationships and eventual
thirty-six construction states remain requirements; a complete painting alone
does not implement those states or native click behavior.

Current source building sheets are subject/material references, not placement
templates. The actual rejected `town-2.png` native screenshot is a negative
composition reference. Original HoMM3 is a rendering reference; the private Riot
Freljord emblem is a shape reference. None is copied as background/game pixels.

Use genuine built-in imagegen painted2D artwork, no Blender/3D, procedural scenery
or render paintovers. Save exact prompts and generated source hashes. Inspect the
whole master at native 800×374 and resolve missing sites or visible geometric
defects before the coordinator's master checkpoint. Do not slice, convert active
assets, edit canonical geometry, publish or launch a native instance before that
checkpoint and explicit coordinator derivation assignment.
