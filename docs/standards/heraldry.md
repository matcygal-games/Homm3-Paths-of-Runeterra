# Regional heraldry

Regional crests help towns and armies read as part of the same place. Include them in town artwork and all fourteen base/upgrade troop variants, including replacement artwork.

## Identity and references

Use the region's documented emblem, with a source URL, access date and reference-image checksum in the asset record. Personal, family and institutional symbols can add detail alongside it. Check that the painted shape remains recognizable at native size, and record redistribution terms for the references and finished artwork.

## Town placement

Banners, gates, carved reliefs and local graffiti are useful places for town crests. Match the surrounding architecture, perspective, materials and weathering. Spread them across suitable locations so the town keeps its identity as buildings are constructed. Check early, partial, complete and coastal views.

## Unit placement

Place troop crests on equipment, shields, cloth, harnesses, collars or ornaments. Each base and upgrade should show a recognizable emblem in an ordinary battle view. Upgrades can develop its material and decoration along with the rest of the equipment.

For animals, spirits and independent communities, explain the marking as a regional association or gameplay adaptation; it need not imply political allegiance or state ownership.

Check the crest through movement, attacks, reactions, turns and death at both facings, as well as adventure sprites and portrait crops. It should follow the surface it is painted or mounted on, with natural changes in perspective and visibility as the unit moves.

## Production and integration

Follow the [art guide](art.md), keeping editable sources, production records and checksums with the asset.

After changing a selected master, rebuild its affected battle, portrait and map outputs through the regional dispatcher. Update selections, recipes and the manifest together, then check the converted files and their appearance in game.

## Review records

The regional coverage record links each crest reference to the selected assets, animation frames and native dimensions. Include its placement, the reviewed build, findings and any remaining corrections. Keep previous reviews with their original build hashes so later artwork can be compared with them. Visual review and redistribution approval are recorded separately.
