# Ionia physical layout candidate

Status: **design proposal**. No admissible final corrected cohort is bound yet.

The first `FRAME-FREEZE-v1.json` was subsequently received and rejected for
admission after visible extraction defects were demonstrated: Protector feather
remnants in the empty sky, wrongly assigned ambient water/Workshop roof pixels
in coast masks, and incomplete whole-institution ownership at the right edge.
That exact cohort and all failed/proposed evidence remain history. Physical
binding waits for the corrected explicit cohort; it must never silently adopt
the rejected v1 frames.

This subtree owns only the semantic geometry contract, binary technical masks,
consumer replay and mutation evidence for the new complete-town-first artwork.
It does not own painted pixels, canonical content/design/recipes or publication.

The shared prompt requires:

> Rear buildings need level landings supporting their complete root, foundation and contact-shadow profiles.

> Record stair exclusions as well as water/solid exclusions, and include a real displaced-building negative control.

> Derive click masks/highlights from selected visible silhouettes.

The unchanged consumer is `scripts/faction_town_layout.py`. Its
`silhouette-v2` contract checks actual all-state alpha silhouettes, full roots,
door/frontage masks, connected approaches and a public path network; depth
approvals may cover only exact supported terrace projections. No approval may
hide an entrance or approach. Optional moored riverboat support is explicitly
bounded to the coastal site and its visible hull/deck.

Before binding a contract, require the source owner's explicit frozen index of
all 35 frames, selected empty terrain, selected complete scene, source site
outline program, actual native depths, and any special coast/Grail changes.
Recheck all pins before and after replay. The 16 ordinary sites, coast and Grail
must cover every canonical state exactly once. Derived frames remain full
800 × 374 canvases at native `x:0,y:0`.

Only the final isolated candidate may be compiled/replayed here. A source
geometry pass does not admit the currently stale full faction stage, validate
unrelated creature artwork or constitute native visual/release acceptance.

Technical mask proposals and their direct RGB/ownership comparisons are kept
under this subtree and matching uniquely named regional reports. Their
`design proposal` status and art-owner review requirement are deliberate. The
long coast underlap proposal is not accepted: a genuine finite landing repair
is being authored to preserve complete real supports without a hidden-contact
waiver. A color-key diagnostic alone is not a faithful silhouette; the latest
visible coast proposal combines actual object envelopes with negative-water
gaps and rejects unverified diagonal envelopes that merely selected river
pixels.

`physical_negative_controls.py` is also prepared but unexecuted. It requires an
actually valid public-consumer baseline. Its four bounded controls distinguish

actual water placement and a real route disconnection. Terrain/support/background
stay unchanged in all three placement controls. Every changed fixture byte is restored
and the baseline is checked again. The authored control coordinates must come
from the final cohort; no speculative control count is reported as an executed
result.

The binder checks the existing native `upgrades` and `builds` relationships
before assigning a replacement family to one site. It prepares every actual

Those visual probes do not claim that removing a prerequisite while retaining
